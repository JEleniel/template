# Evaluate Document

Read through the attached document(s) and evaluate for completeness, clarity, conciseness, followability, and errors.

## Devlierables

- A prioritized list of feedback items, including specific locations and suggested fixes or adjusments.
- Suggestions for improving the document's overall quality, such as restructuring or rephrasing sections for better clarity and flow.

## Out of Scope

- Do not include a list of positive aspects or aspects that need no improvement. Focus on providing constructive criticism and actionable feedback for improvement.
- Fix typographical and grammatic errors, but do not make any other changes to the document. The goal is to evaluate and provide feedback, not to edit or rewrite the document.
